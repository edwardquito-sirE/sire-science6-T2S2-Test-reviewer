Competency 1: Roles of Organisms in Food Webs

TOS Weight: 33% | Items 1–6

1. Producers (Autotrophs)

Organisms that create their own food using sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide through Photosynthesis. They serve as the base of all ecological food webs.

Examples: Rice Plants, Corn, Mango Trees, Green Algae, Grass.

2. Consumers (Heterotrophs)

Organisms that cannot make their own food and must feed on other living organisms for energy.

  • Herbivores (Primary): Eat producers directly (e.g., Caterpillars, Grasshoppers, Carabaos).
  • Carnivores (Secondary/Tertiary): Eat other animals (e.g., Frogs, Snakes, Philippine Eagles).
  • Omnivores: Eat both plants and animals (e.g., Chickens, Humans, Monkeys).

3. Scavengers

Animals that feed on dead bodies and decaying animal remains that they did not kill themselves. They keep natural habitats clean.

Examples: Vultures, Crows feeding on farm carcasses, Hyenas, Crows.

4. Decomposers

Microorganisms and fungi that chemically break down dead organic matter, returning vital minerals and nutrients back into the soil for producers.

Examples: Mushrooms (Fungi), Soil Bacteria, Earthworms.

Competency 2: Ecological Relationships in Nature

TOS Weight: 33% | Items 7–13

Mutualism (+ / +)

A symbiotic interaction where both species benefit from each other.

🐝 Bee & Flower: Bee gets sweet nectar; flower gets pollinated!

Commensalism (+ / 0)

One species benefits, while the host is neither helped nor harmed.

🌿 Orchid on Tree: Orchid gets high sunlight; tree suffers zero harm!

Parasitism (+ / -)

One organism (parasite) benefits by living in/on a host and causing harm.

🦟 Mosquito & Human: Mosquito gains blood; human suffers itch and risk of illness.

Predation

One organism (predator) actively hunts, kills, and eats another organism (prey).

🦅 Eagle & Flying Fox: Philippine Eagle hunts flying foxes for sustenance.

Competition

Organisms compete with one another for limited resources like water, light, nutrients, or space.

🌾 Weeds & Rice: Weeds compete with rice crops for soil minerals and sunlight.

Competency 3: Biotic & Abiotic Ecosystem Components

TOS Weight: 33% | Items 14–20

Biotic Factors

All the living or once-living organisms in an environment.

  • 🌱 Green Plants, Mangroves, Rice Crops, Ferns
  • 🐸 Frogs, Caterpillars, Snakes, Carabao, Mudskippers
  • 🍄 Mushrooms, Soil Bacteria, Earthworms

Abiotic Factors

All the non-living physical and chemical elements that influence organism survival.

  • ☀️ Light: Drives photosynthesis energy production.
  • 💧 Water: Vital for cellular processes & transport.
  • 🌡️ Temperature: Governs biological metabolic rates.
  • 🪴 Soil Type: Loam, Clay, or Sand supplying essential minerals.
